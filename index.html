Virginia Cooling Deserts &
Heat Safety Matrix

This interactive spatial analysis evaluates environmental equity and emergency heat safety across Virginia by overlaying household air conditioning deficits against vehicular accessibility to public cooling infrastructure. By modeling 10-minute street network drive times around municipal public libraries, the web application identifies high-priority "cooling deserts"—vulnerable communities with elevated household AC deficits and limited rapid access to climate-controlled public facilities during extreme heat events.
